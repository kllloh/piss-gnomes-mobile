PISS GNOMES

Last gnome standing

Twenty gnomes. One small neon-lit island: forest, ice or desert. Soak a gnome and they join your team. Pee is your only weapon, and the mist is closing in.

HOW TO PLAY

1 · THE GOAL

  • Be on the last team standing. Twenty gnomes start alone. Soak a gnome and they join your team (cyan on the map), up to 5 per team, and your streams pass through your teammates. The first time you are soaked you go to the gulag: a floating arena for a 1v1 against the next gnome to be soaked (or the guard). Win and you respawn. Lose and whoever soaked you takes you onto their team (if it has room), otherwise you are out. The mist eliminates for good. You choose where to spawn on the map; everybody else is spread out around the island.
  • Pee is your only weapon. Each hit takes health.
  • Health creeps back after 4 quiet seconds, but not while you are burning or melting.
  • A pink mist closes in: stay inside the circle on the minimap.

2 · PHONE OR TABLET

  • Left side: drag to walk. The blue button is JUMP.
  • Right side: drag anywhere to look.
  • Hold PEE to pee (slide your thumb on it to aim). It also fires by itself when an enemy is in range and near your crosshair: the AUTO button next to the sound button turns that off.

COMPUTER

  • WASD walk, mouse look (click the game to capture it), hold click or F to pee, Space jumps, Esc pauses.

3 · AIMING

  • Look up to throw your stream farther. The glowing ring on the ground is where it lands.
  • Mushroom stems block it.
  • Your bladder empties as you pee and refills when you stop. Run dry and you wait for it to come back.

4 · POTIONS

  • Walk into a glowing bottle for 25 seconds of:
  • CHILI sets them on fire
  • ICE slows, then freezes them
  • ACID melts them and they take extra damage
  • WHITE MONSTER a rare white can: infinite bladder and a double stream for 20 s
  • Grab different ones to mix them: Thermal Shock, Napalm, Cryo-Acid, or all three for the Rainbow Cocktail (it makes them dizzy).

5 · FARTING BEETLES

  • Little blocky beetles wander about and fart out a bonus. Grab it before it vanishes (16 s):
  • SPEED HEALTH BOUNCE BIG GULP

NATHANIEL

  • The tall metalhead in glasses (a gold diamond on the map). Say hi: walk up close to him and he gives you Wizard Mode: 20 seconds where nothing can hurt you.

6 · THE ISLAND

  • Hills, a river, lakes and the sea. Wading slows you down and refills your bladder; the deep sea is too deep to enter.
  • Twelve named places: Pee-nacle Peak, Soggy Bottom, Stonehedge, Tinkle Town, Gnome Depot, Chili Fields, Puddle Pond, Dribble Falls, Sunbathing Shores, Ice Wee Glacier, Acid Swamp and Wee Woods.
  • Tap the minimap (or press M) for the big map with all the names.

7 · MODES AND TIPS

  • Modes change the rules: Disco (faster, stronger streams), Goth (dark, no healing), Turbo (everybody runs and pees faster), Upside Down (the world flips, and so do your controls), Moon (low gravity: huge jumps and streams that fly three times as far), Slippery (you skid about like on ice) and Inferno (every stream is chilli pee: everybody burns).
  • A new mode every 20 seconds, and now and then a White Monster drop rains cans all over the island.
  • Colourful dots on the minimap are bonuses. White is you.
  • The world only goes psychedelic when you are badly hurt.
  • Wear headphones: every sound comes from where it happens.
