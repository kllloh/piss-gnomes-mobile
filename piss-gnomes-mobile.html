Ten gnomes. One big cheerful mushroom forest. Pee is your only weapon, and the mist is closing in.

HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

  • Be the last gnome standing out of 10.
  • Pee is your only weapon. Each hit takes health.
  • Health creeps back after 4 quiet seconds, but not while you are burning or melting.

ON A PHONE OR TABLET

  • Left side: drag to walk. JUMP is the blue button.
  • Right side: drag anywhere to look.
  • Hold the yellow PEE button to pee. Slide your thumb on it to aim while you pee.

ON A COMPUTER

  • WASD walk, mouse look (click the game to capture it).
  • Hold click or F to pee, Space to jump.
  • Esc pauses.

AIMING

  • Look up to throw your stream farther. The glowing ring on the ground is where it lands.
  • Mushroom stems block it. Bladder empties as you pee and refills when you stop.

POTIONS

  • Walk into a glowing bottle for 25 seconds of:
  • CHILI sets them on fire
  • ICE slows, then freezes them
  • ACID melts them and they take extra damage
  • Grab different ones to mix them for Thermal Shock, Napalm, Cryo-Acid or the Rainbow Cocktail.

FARTING BEETLES

  • Big blocky beetles wander about and fart out a bonus. Grab it before it vanishes (16 s):
  • SPEED HEALTH BOUNCE BIG GULP

NATHANIEL

  • The tall metalhead in glasses (a gold diamond on the map). Touch him and he gives you Wizard Mode: 20 seconds where nothing can hurt you.

THE MIST AND THE MODES

  • A pink mist closes in. Stay inside the circle on the minimap.
  • Random modes change the rules: Disco (faster, stronger streams), Goth (dark, no healing), Bogan (big bladder, drunk aim).

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Colourful dots on the minimap are bonuses. White is you.
  • The world only goes psychedelic when you are badly hurt.
  • Wear headphones: every sound comes from where it happens.
